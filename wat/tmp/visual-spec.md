# Visual Spec — Load Manager Tutorial

## Palette (Logistics Suite candy)

- Pink primary: `#d4738c` (hover `#b85775`)
- Pink soft: `#e8b4c8` / `#fdf2f6`
- Purple: `#9b7bb8`
- Mint: `#7bc8b5`
- Peach: `#e8a87c`
- Gold: `#c8a062`
- Cocoa (ink): `#2c2823`

## Fonts
- Display + body: **DM Sans** 400/500/600/700/800
- Mono accents (kickers, load #, timestamps, code): **JetBrains Mono** 400/500/600

## Lower-third format
```
+---------------------------------------+
|  CH 03 · 01  |  Pulling today's loads |
|   (mono)     |   (DM Sans 28/700)     |
+---------------------------------------+
```
- Background: glass pill `rgba(255,253,249,0.88)` with `backdrop-filter:blur(14px)`
- Border: 1px `#f0d0dc`

## Callout arrows
- Stroke: pink `#d4738c`, 2.4px round-cap
- Arrowhead: 8px filled triangle
- Label pill: cream `#fdfaf4`, 1px pink-border hairline, 11px DM Sans 600

## B-roll list (cues for the editor)
1. Hover over **Needs Driver** stat tab — pulse the red border
2. Click ✨ **Smart Shuffle** — slow-mo the list re-sort
3. Open a load card — zoom on the row of buttons (Driver / In Depth / G2G)
4. Type into the **AI prompt box** in the template editor — show the cursor blink
5. **Mode toggle** flip — Shipping → Deliveries, slo-mo on the switch
6. **Calendar view** — month grid, colored dots
7. **Fleet Map** — Leaflet zoom from continent down to city cluster
8. **Weather badge** macro — sun + 78° + city
9. **Kimbo bear** opening — cute bounce
10. **Workflow Benefits** modal — side-by-side time comparison

## On-app theme reminder
The Load Manager *app itself* renders in Bay & Bay's navy/gold theme (`#0B1120` background, `#C9A96E` accents). That's by design — it's the in-house tool branding. The tutorial *wrapper* (intro card, outro, callouts, thumbnail) is the candy Suite palette. Do NOT re-skin the app to match Suite; the contrast is the visual story.

## Cut rhythm
- Cold open: 4-second cuts (tension)
- Body chapters: 6-10 second cuts (instructional)
- Demo moments: hold longer, let the click breathe
- Outro: 3-second beats, music swell
