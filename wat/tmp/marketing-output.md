## Trend angle (why this lands in 2026)
Freight ops YouTube in late 2025 / early 2026 has tipped toward **single-operator tools**. The era of "we'll license a $200/seat TMS module" is over for the 5-50 loads/day segment. Brokers are building (or asking AI to build) their own HTML dashboards on top of their existing TMS. Load Manager is exactly that: a real working broker's real working dashboard, no SaaS, no login. The trend is "show me the actual file, not the marketing site." This video opens the actual file.

Also: DeepSeek visibility is rising. Most freight YouTube hasn't shown a DeepSeek-powered chatbot drilling into live load data yet. Kimbo + Smart Shuffle is a novelty hook for the AI-curious wing of the audience.
