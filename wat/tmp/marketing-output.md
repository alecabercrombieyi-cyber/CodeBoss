## 5. Trend angle (freight YouTube, mid-2026)

The freight YouTube niche right now is dominated by three trend buckets:
1. **Rate trends and capacity** (Ken Adamo / Craig Fuller / DAT iQ explainers) — broad market commentary.
2. **Broker-vs-driver content** (Trucker Trent style, dispatch-heavy) — operational drama.
3. **Tool reviews & "I built X" content** — sparse, but high-CTR when the tool solves a named pain.

Bolt Post fits squarely in bucket 3, which is *underserved* by actual brokers (most "I built X" content in freight is from outside vendors). **The trend angle: "I built it myself, in my browser, and it's free."** That's distinctive because:
- It signals the creator does the work daily (credibility moat).
- It rides the broader "indie devs / single-file apps" wave that's getting traction outside freight.
- It positions against the heavy enterprise TMS players whose tools start at $25k/seat/year.

## 6. Target viewer

- **Primary:** US freight broker, 25–45, 2–10 years in the industry, posts 20–100 loads per day on DAT, uses Revenova or another Salesforce-based TMS. Already frustrated with manual copying. Has heard of bookmarklets but doesn't write code.
- **Secondary:** Broker team leads / shop owners evaluating "should I let my brokers use this." They want to see: free, no backend, no data leaves the browser, no IT approval needed.
- **Tertiary:** Indie freight tool builders looking at the patterns (single-file HTML, native value setters for React/Angular form auto-fill, hidden-iframe report download).

**What they want to leave the video knowing:**
1. When to use Bolt Post vs Mass Post (answer: pricing vs dispatching).
2. How the bookmarklet works enough to trust it on their DAT account.
3. That the multi-stop feature is the unique value, not just "Mass Post lite."
4. Where to download it (free, single file).
