## Trend angle

1. **"Broker layoffs / industry shrinking"** — anxiety content. Pair it with: practical tools that make individual brokers more valuable.
2. **"AI is replacing brokers"** — counter-positioning. Pair it with: tools built BY brokers, not AI hype.
