## Trend angle (Q1–Q2 2026 freight-YouTube)

Three trends compound right now:

1. **Carrier-fraud / double-brokering content is at peak attention.** FreightCaviar, FreightWaves Fraud Files, and Freight 360 are all producing weekly content on identity-swap and double-broker schemes. CarrierVet is a defensive tool against exactly that, but the emotional cover ("don't be the broker who got tricked") is what gets the click.
2. **"Free broker tool" videos are converting better than "best practices" videos.** Brokers are tired of theory and want software they can open today. CarrierVet runs in-browser, no signup — that's the description headline.
3. **The "built by an actual broker, not a SaaS founder" credibility angle is undefeated.** Lean into the Bay & Bay tenure and the candy/sweet-side-of-freight aesthetic — it differentiates from McLeod, Banyan, and the enterprise pack on the YouTube grid.

How this video rides them: thumbnail leads with "VET A CARRIER IN 10 SECONDS" + a red BLOCKED stamp (fraud anxiety + speed promise), title leads with "Stop Getting Double-Brokered" (the highest-CTR phrase in freight-YouTube right now), and the cold open names a specific dollar-figure loss before mentioning the product. Then the video earns the click with an honest walkthrough that explicitly says "this is NOT a fraud-scoring AI" in the first two minutes — buyers in 2026 are inoculated against over-promising and reward honesty.
