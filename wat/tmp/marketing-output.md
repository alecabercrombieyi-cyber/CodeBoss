## Trend angle
Freight YouTube is dominated three ways right now:
1. **"How dispatchers use ChatGPT"** — high searches but low watch-time because the videos are mostly screenshots
2. **TMS / load-board demos** — sponsored, polished, and obviously sales
3. **Owner-op vlogs** — entertainment, not workflow

The opening is in **#1**: viewers want concrete AI-for-freight content but the existing videos don't show actual broker workflow. Freight Matcher slots there with a real, free, single-file tool whose AI use is invisible (the parser does it under the hood). The differentiator is the **map payoff** — every competing tool is a spreadsheet; this one *looks* like the work. The thumbnail leans into that with the literal fire pin.

Secondary trend: **"I built this for myself"** content is over-performing on LinkedIn for freight in 2026. Cross-post a 60-second cut to LinkedIn the day the video drops, captioned with the ROI math.

## Target audience
**Primary**: Mid-sized brokerage dispatchers and freight brokers (3–50 person shops) who do daily inbox sweeps of carrier availability and run loads in a TMS like Revenova or McLeod. They're spending 45–60 minutes a morning on email triage and they know it. They are *not* on YouTube to "learn AI" — they are on YouTube searching for tools.

**Secondary**: Owner-operators and small carrier dispatchers who want to see what brokers are matching against — gives them context for why their availability emails get / don't get attention.

**Tertiary**: Indie freight founders / freight-tech builders who'll fork the source.
