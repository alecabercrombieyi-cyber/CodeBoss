# 03 — Marketing workflow

## Goal
Position the CarrierVet tutorial so it gets clicked by freight brokers spooked by the 2026 wave of double-brokering and carrier fraud, and ranks for the right freight-YouTube searches.

## Target viewer
- Freight broker, dispatcher, or carrier sales rep (1–10 years in)
- Watches freight-YouTube content like Freight 360, DAT One demos, FreightCaviar, FreightWaves Fraud Files
- Their pain: getting double-brokered, identity-swapped MCs, blowing a tier-3 booking on a carrier with no lane history, hand-checking Highway + Carrier411 + FMCSA one tab at a time

## Constraints
- Title: 5 A/B options. Each must be honest (no clickbait), under 70 chars, contain at least one freight keyword (carrier, broker, vet, MC, fraud, Highway). Lean on the "avoid getting double-brokered" / "verify carriers in 10 seconds" angle.
- Description: 2–3 paragraphs, then chapter timestamps. First paragraph is the hook for both humans and YouTube.
- Tags: 15 tags, prioritized — broad freight tags first, niche tool tags last
- Hook: word-for-word 15-second opener. Must include a specific dollar figure (typical double-broker loss runs $3K–$25K per load) or time savings to anchor the value.
- Trend angle: short paragraph on what's working on freight-YouTube right now (carrier fraud, double-brokering, identity-swap, FMCSA hot-truck flags are all 2026 hot topics) and how this rides it.

## Deliverable
`wat/tmp/marketing-output.md` containing all of the above, clearly sectioned.
