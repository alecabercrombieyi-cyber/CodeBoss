# 03 — Marketing workflow

## Goal
Position the Mass Post tutorial so it gets clicked by freight brokers and ranks for the right freight-YouTube searches.

## Target viewer
- Freight broker, dispatcher, or carrier sales rep (1–10 years in)
- Watches freight-YouTube content like Freight 360, DAT One demos, FreightWaves How-Tos
- Their pain: re-typing rates between DAT and Revenova, manual posting, slow rate research before a call

## Constraints
- Title: 5 A/B options. Each must be honest (no clickbait), under 70 chars, contain at least one freight keyword (DAT, broker, post, rate, Revenova)
- Description: 2–3 paragraphs, then chapter timestamps. First paragraph is the hook for both humans and YouTube.
- Tags: 15 tags, prioritized — broad freight tags first, niche tool tags last
- Hook: word-for-word 15-second opener. Must include a specific dollar figure or time savings to anchor the value.
- Trend angle: short paragraph on what's working on freight-YouTube right now and how this rides it.

## Deliverable
`wat/tmp/marketing-output.md` containing all of the above, clearly sectioned.
