# 03 — Marketing Workflow

## Goal
Position Bolt Post for freight brokers scrolling YouTube on lunch break. The viewer must understand within 15 seconds what the tool does and why it's faster than what they do now.

## Constraints
- Title A/Bs: lead with the speed/efficiency angle. Avoid clickbait that doesn't pay off in the video.
- Description: front-load the value prop; include time stamps for chapters.
- 15 tags: mix of high-volume freight terms ("freight broker", "load posting", "DAT load board") with niche tooling terms ("bulk post", "TMS automation", "Revenova").
- Hook: opens on a pain (manual posting), pivots to relief in <15 sec.

## Done when
- `wat/tmp/marketing-output.md` contains all five sections fully filled (titles, description, tags, hook, trend angle, target viewer).
- Each title under 70 characters.
- Trend angle ties to current freight YouTube (rate trends, capacity, broker burnout).
