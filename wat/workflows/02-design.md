# 02 — Design Workflow

## Goal
Bolt Post's identity is *speed* and *electric blue* (the source uses `--bolt: #4D8DFF` and `--electric: #A78BFA` on a deep navy `#0A0E1A` background — that's the app's native skin). The tutorial package's design must honor that *and* sit harmoniously inside the candy-pink Suite brand so viewers feel the connection to Mass Post and the rest of the showroom.

## Constraints
- Thumbnail: 1280x720 SVG. Must be readable as a 320px wide preview tile.
- Use Suite candy palette as the chrome (pink #d4738c, cream #faf7f2, gold #c8a062) and Bolt's electric blue + lightning shapes as the energy.
- Lightning bolt is the visual cue, not generic "speed lines". Single dominant bolt > many small ones.
- Title cards must be reproducible in a video editor (specify exact text, fonts, palette tokens).

## Done when
- Thumbnail SVG validates and is visually scannable at 320px.
- Title cards spec covers: cold-open card, chapter cards, "TRY IT" CTA card, outro card.
- Visual spec covers screen-recording tips (resolution, cursor highlighter, zoom-in callouts).
