# Workflow: Design

## Goal
Produce a brand-aligned thumbnail, on-screen title cards, and a visual spec for screen recording.

## Outputs
- `wat/tmp/thumbnail.svg` — 1280x720, candy palette, soap/sparkle/clean-data visual
- `wat/tmp/title-cards.md` — list of on-screen lower-thirds and chapter title cards
- `wat/tmp/visual-spec.md` — screen recording guidance (resolution, zoom level, cursor highlights, B-roll moments)

## Brand
- Palette: candy pink (#d4738c / #b85775), cream (#faf7f2), purple (#9b7bb8), mint (#7bc8b5), peach (#e8a87c), gold (#c8a062)
- Fonts: DM Sans, JetBrains Mono
- Tagline: "Built by brokers · for brokers"

## Important
The Data Wash app itself uses a green/emerald dark theme (#10b981) — NOT the candy palette. The thumbnail and title cards stay candy-branded (Logistics Suite house style); the in-app screen recordings will visibly show emerald. Acknowledge this contrast in `visual-spec.md` — recommend a candy-pink ribbon/lower-third overlay during in-app footage to reinforce brand.

## Thumbnail must include
- App name "Data Wash"
- A visual metaphor for cleaning sensitive data (bubble, sparkle, before/after blur)
- A bold value claim ("Hide PII in 2 min" or "Share Safe CSVs")
- Logistics Suite candy palette dominant
