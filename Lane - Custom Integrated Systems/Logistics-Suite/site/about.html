Logistics Suite is a set of quiet, practical tools for the freight brokers who actually move the loads. No buzzwords. No 30-minute demos. Just the stuff you wish your other software already did.

Our story

We got tired of waiting on software that didn't know the job.

After years of bouncing between load boards, TMS tabs, spreadsheets, and Slack threads, we started building the tools we wished we had. A load manager that opens in a second. A freight matcher that understands lanes. A data-wash that handles the junk in broker data so you don't have to.

Every tool in this suite started as a fix for something that was slowing us down on a Tuesday afternoon.

What we believe

Four words. We judge every feature against them.

Integrated — your tools should know each other. A load you created in one place should be visible everywhere else, without a sync job.

Simple — if it takes a manual, it's broken.

Controlled — your data, your account, your choice. No dark patterns, no surprise bills.

Efficient — minutes, not hours. Every tool has a stopwatch we measure against.
