## 5. Trend angle (freight YouTube, mid-2026)

The freight YouTube niche right now lives in three buckets: rate-trend commentary (DAT iQ / FreightWaves), broker-vs-driver storytelling, and tool-review / "I built X" content. Bolt Post fits cleanly in bucket 3, which is *underserved by actual brokers* — most tool content in freight comes from outside vendors selling something.

Distinctive trend angle: **"I built it myself, in my browser, and it's free."** It signals the creator works the job daily, rides the broader "single-file indie apps" wave that's getting traction outside freight, and positions Bolt Post against enterprise TMS players whose tools start at $25k/seat/year.

## 6. Target viewer

- **Primary:** US freight broker, 25–45, 2–10 years in industry, posts 20–100 loads/day on DAT, uses Revenova or another Salesforce-based TMS. Frustrated with manual copying. Has heard of bookmarklets but doesn't write code.
- **Secondary:** Broker team leads / shop owners evaluating "should I let my brokers use this." Want to see: free, no backend, no data leaves the browser, no IT approval needed.
- **Tertiary:** Indie freight tool builders looking at the patterns (single-file HTML, native value setters for React/Angular form auto-fill, hidden-iframe report download).

**What they leave the video knowing:**
1. When to use Bolt Post vs Mass Post (pricing vs dispatching).
2. How the bookmarklet works enough to trust it on their DAT account.
3. That multi-stop reply generation is the unique value, not just "Mass Post lite."
4. Where to download it (free, single file).
