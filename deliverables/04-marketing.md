## Trend angle
Freight YouTube right now is dominated three ways: (1) "How dispatchers use ChatGPT" with high searches but low watch-time (screenshot-heavy), (2) sponsored TMS/load-board demos that read as sales, (3) owner-op vlogs (entertainment, not workflow). The opening is in **#1**: viewers want concrete AI-for-freight content but the existing videos don't show actual broker workflow. Freight Matcher slots there with a real, free, single-file tool where the AI is invisible (parser under the hood). The differentiator is the **map payoff** — every competing tool is a spreadsheet; this one *looks* like the work. Thumbnail leans into the literal fire pin.

Secondary trend: **"I built this for myself"** content over-performs on LinkedIn for freight in 2026. Cross-post a 60-second cut to LinkedIn the day the video drops, captioned with the ROI math.

## Target audience
**Primary**: Mid-sized brokerage dispatchers and freight brokers (3–50 person shops) running TMS systems like Revenova or McLeod, spending 45–60 minutes a morning on email triage. They are *not* on YouTube to "learn AI" — they are searching for *tools*.

**Secondary**: Owner-operators and small carrier dispatchers wanting to see what brokers match against — context for why their availability emails get / don't get attention.

**Tertiary**: Indie freight founders / freight-tech builders who'll fork the source.
