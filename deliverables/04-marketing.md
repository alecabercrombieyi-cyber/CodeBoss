## Trend Angle
Freight ops YouTube in late 2025 / early 2026 has tipped toward **single-operator tools**. The era of "we'll license a $200/seat TMS module" is over for the 5-50 loads/day segment. Brokers are building (or asking AI to build) their own HTML dashboards on top of their existing TMS. Load Manager is exactly that — a real working broker's real working dashboard, no SaaS, no login.

Secondary angle: **DeepSeek visibility is rising.** Most freight YouTube hasn't shown a DeepSeek-powered chatbot drilling into live load data yet. Kimbo + Smart Shuffle is a novelty hook for the AI-curious wing of the audience.

## Target Viewer
- **Primary:** Freight brokers and dispatchers at 5-100-person brokerages running Salesforce / Revenova / McLeod / Aljex. 5-50 loads/day per broker. Frustrated with their TMS's native dashboard.
- **Secondary:** Owner-operator dispatchers managing their own book. They want the calendar + fleet map most.
- **Tertiary:** Freight-tech curious (devs, founders, ops leaders) watching to see what an in-house broker tool actually looks like under the hood.
