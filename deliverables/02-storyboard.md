- **Audio:** Subtle synth swell. Lightning crack SFX at the title cut. VO over the swell.
- **Title card cue:** at 0:14, slam in the "BOLT POST · EP 02" wordmark, hold 4 seconds.

## Scene 2 · 0:18–1:00 · WHAT THIS IS
- **Shot:** Talking-head insert in PIP corner if available; otherwise stay on the Bolt Post UI with a "Mass Post → Bolt Post" comparison title card. Side-by-side stills of both apps at 60% opacity.
- **Audio:** Music bed continues, ducked -14 dB.
- **B-roll:** Quick 2-frame flash to Mass Post (3 tabs visible) → cut back to Bolt Post (no tabs). Establishes the difference visually.

- **Title card** at 6:33: `CHAPTER 04 · COPY → FILL DAT`.
